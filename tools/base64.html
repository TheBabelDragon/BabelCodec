BabelCodec · Tools · Base64

Base64 Encoder and Decoder

Convert between text/bytes and Base64. Runs entirely in the browser.

Explanation

Base64 maps binary data to an ASCII alphabet of 64 characters. It is an encoding for transport, not encryption or hashing.

Example: Hi → SGk=
